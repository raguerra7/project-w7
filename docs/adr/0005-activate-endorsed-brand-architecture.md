# ADR-0005: Activate the Endorsed Brand Architecture

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-08-03

Owner: Rafael da Silva Guerra

---

## Context

W7 needs an active identity model before additional initiatives or visual assets are created. The Brand Architecture already described initiatives as independently named brands with a visible relationship to W7, but remained marked `Draft` and used the mixed term "endorsed sub-brands." Its role also needed to remain independent of WarTips so the Identity layer would govern initiatives rather than derive its authority from one downstream example.

## Constitutional Assessment

- **Why should this exist?** Initiatives need a consistent way to express their relationship to W7 without losing their own identity.
- **Who benefits?** Initiative leads, designers, contributors and audiences gain a clear ownership and naming model.
- **Will it remain valuable over time?** Yes. The relationship model is independent of a particular visual style, product or technology.
- **Does it strengthen the W7 ecosystem?** Yes. Trust and recognition can compound across initiatives while each remains appropriate to its own audience.

## Decision

Activate version 1.1.0 of the Brand Architecture and adopt endorsed brands as W7's identity model. Every initiative keeps its own name, voice and visual expression while maintaining a visible connection to W7. Detailed visual identity remains deferred until a concrete use case exists.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Use one monolithic W7 brand.** Rejected because unrelated initiatives may serve different audiences and require distinct voices.
- **Use fully independent brands.** Rejected because the relationship to W7 would be invisible and trust could not compound across the ecosystem.
- **Keep the architecture as Draft until visual assets exist.** Rejected because the brand relationship must guide those assets when they are created; it does not depend on them.
- **Derive the model specifically from WarTips.** Rejected because a shared Identity layer must remain valid for initiatives that do not resemble the first one.

## Consequences

New initiatives must preserve a visible connection to W7 but have room to develop identities suited to their audiences. Future visual identity work has a stable relationship model to express. W7 accepts the ongoing responsibility to keep the endorsement clear and consistent across initiative touchpoints.
