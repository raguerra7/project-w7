# Visual Language

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Active

Owner: Rafael da Silva Guerra

---

## Purpose

This document defines the visual principles used across W7 artifacts without replacing asset-specific design files.

---

## Context

W7 should communicate engineering, precision, elegance, simplicity, and long-term intent. A principle-led language keeps future assets coherent while leaving room for initiative-specific expression.

---

## Direction

The W7 visual language is minimal, structured, and human. It uses clear grids, generous negative space, strong hierarchy, and a restrained core palette.

## Symbol Direction

The endorsed direction is a circular W7 monogram inspired subtly by balance: technology and people, learning and teaching, strategy and execution, present and future. It must not reproduce the yin-yang symbol literally. The final production mark requires a dedicated asset review before release.

## Composition

- Prefer simple geometry and visible alignment.
- Use whitespace as structure, not leftover space.
- Keep decorative elements subordinate to content.
- Use diagrams to explain systems, not to suggest technical sophistication.
- Prefer authentic photography or functional illustration over generic technology imagery.

## Avoid

- exaggerated neon, cyberpunk, or gamer aesthetics;
- ornamental circuit patterns and generic AI imagery;
- dense dashboards used as decoration;
- metallic effects that reduce legibility; and
- motion without informational or experiential purpose.

## Motion

Motion should explain hierarchy, change, or continuity. It is brief, calm, respects reduced-motion preferences, and never blocks access to content.

## Asset Governance

This document governs direction, not finished logo files. Production assets must record format, clear space, minimum size, contrast variants, accessibility review, and approval before becoming canonical.
