## Purpose

This document defines how W7 communicates while allowing each initiative to adapt to its own audience.

---

## Context

A coherent identity depends as much on language as visuals. W7's voice remains stable; tone changes with context.

---

## Voice

W7 is clear, thoughtful, precise, generous, and grounded.

- **Clear:** lead with meaning and use the simplest accurate language.
- **Thoughtful:** include relevant context and acknowledge trade-offs.
- **Precise:** distinguish facts, decisions, plans, and possibilities.
- **Generous:** help the reader act; do not use expertise to create distance.
- **Grounded:** avoid hype, unsupported certainty, and borrowed prestige.

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Governing documentation | Direct, durable, unambiguous |
| Technical guidance | Practical, specific, evidence-led |
| Teaching | Patient, encouraging, never patronizing |
| Incident or correction | Calm, accountable, focused on learning |
| Public brand communication | Confident, restrained, human |

## Language Rules

- Prefer active voice and concrete verbs.
- Explain acronyms on first use unless the audience is explicitly specialist.
- Do not claim leadership, innovation, or excellence; demonstrate them through the work.
- Use “we” only when speaking for W7 or an identified team, not to manufacture scale.
- “Enjoy the Journey” is W7's closing philosophy; use it intentionally, not as filler on every page.

## Initiative Autonomy

An initiative may define a more specific voice. It must remain honest, accessible, and compatible with the values it inherits from W7.
