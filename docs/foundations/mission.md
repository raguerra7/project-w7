## Context

The Constitution defines W7's authority and the Vision defines its destination. The Mission connects both to present action without prescribing a particular company form, product, or technology.

---

## Mission

W7 builds meaningful initiatives through engineering, technology, design, and education.

It turns knowledge into durable systems, useful products, and clear learning experiences. It does so with intention, shares what can help others, and keeps technology accountable to the people it serves.

## Mission in Practice

W7 fulfills this mission by:

- capturing and refining knowledge before it is lost;
- creating initiatives around a clear, lasting purpose;
- applying engineering discipline to both technical and non-technical work;
- teaching from real experience rather than manufactured authority;
- designing systems that can evolve without abandoning their principles; and
- measuring progress by usefulness, learning, and positive impact rather than activity alone.

## Boundaries

The mission does not require W7 to become a particular kind of organization. It may support open-source work, education, consulting, products, research, or other forms when they satisfy the Constitution and the Initiative Framework.
