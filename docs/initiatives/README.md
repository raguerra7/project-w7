## Purpose

This document is the canonical registry of initiatives recognized within W7.

---

## Context

Ideas and possible future names are not initiatives. Registration makes status, purpose, ownership, and repository boundaries explicit.

---

## Registered Initiatives

| Initiative | Purpose | Status | Products |
|---|---|---|---|
| [WarTips](wartips/README.md) | Share practical technical knowledge grounded in real experience | Active | None formally registered |

## Candidates

Home Lab, Camarim, Academy, and Labs are names or areas of interest, not approved initiatives. They gain official status only through the Initiative Framework and a completed registration document.

## Registration Rule

Each initiative has exactly one directory under `docs/initiatives/`, a named owner, an explicit lifecycle state, and links to any separately owned repositories. Products are registered under exactly one initiative.
